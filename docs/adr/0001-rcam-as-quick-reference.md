# Treat RCAM output as a quick reference

A220Tools will present RCAM-related takeoff and landing wind guidance as a quick-reference aid, not as an approved performance or dispatch calculator. This keeps the feature useful for rapidly cross-referencing changing ATIS, METAR, AWOS, or ATC wind reports while preserving the boundary that pilots must verify operational limits against approved aircraft and company sources.
